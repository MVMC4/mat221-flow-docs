A power series $\sum c_n(x-a)^n$ converges inside a radius, diverges outside, and requires separate endpoint tests.

## Radius and interval

Apply the ratio test to the absolute value and solve $|x-a|<R$. Then substitute $a-R$ and $a+R$ into the original numeric series.

For $\sum x^n/n$, the ratio gives $R=1$. At $x=1$ the harmonic series diverges; at $x=-1$ the alternating harmonic series converges. The interval is $[-1,1)$.

## Term-by-term calculus

Inside the radius, differentiate or integrate term by term. The radius stays the same, although endpoint behavior can change. This manufactures familiar series such as $-\ln(1-x)$ and $\tan^{-1}x$.

<div className="soft-callout"><strong>Transfer.</strong> For $\sum (x-3)^n/2^n$, the centre is $3$, not $0$, and the radius is $2$.</div>
