title: Partial Fractions
description: Decompose rational functions into integrable pieces.

Factor the denominator, write the complete template, solve the constants, integrate each piece, and differentiate to check.

## Templates

| Denominator factor | Required terms |
| --- | --- |
| $x-a$ | $A/(x-a)$ |
| $(x-a)^m$ | $A_1/(x-a)+\cdots+A_m/(x-a)^m$ |
| Irreducible quadratic | $(Bx+C)/(x^2+bx+c)$ |

If the numerator degree is at least the denominator degree, divide first.

## Worked example

For $\int (x+3)/(x(x-1)^2)\,dx$, write:

$$
\frac{x+3}{x(x-1)^2}=\frac{A}{x}+\frac{B}{x-1}+\frac{C}{(x-1)^2}.
$$

Clear denominators, use $x=0$ and $x=1$ for quick constants, then match one coefficient for the remaining constant. Integrate the logarithmic terms and the repeated-factor power.

<div className="soft-callout"><strong>Transfer.</strong> For $1/(x^2-4)$, factor first. The two linear factors produce two logarithms that can be combined.</div>

<div className="soft-callout"><strong>Common error.</strong> A repeated factor needs every power, and an irreducible quadratic needs a linear numerator.</div>
