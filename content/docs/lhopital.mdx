---
title: L'Hopital's Rule
description: Compare rates of change only for genuine indeterminate quotients.
---

L'Hopital's rule is a rate comparison, not an algebra shortcut. Verify $0/0$ or $\infty/\infty$ first.

## Algorithm

1. Substitute and classify the form.
2. Differentiate numerator and denominator separately.
3. Re-evaluate the new limit.
4. Repeat only while the form remains indeterminate.

For $\lim_{x\to0}(e^x-1-x)/x^2$, the first derivative still gives $0/0$. A second application gives $e^x/2$, so the limit is $1/2$.
